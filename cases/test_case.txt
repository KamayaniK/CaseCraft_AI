ACME FOODS: EXPANSION DECISION

ACME Foods is a mid-sized packaged food company operating in India.

The company has experienced steady revenue growth over the last three years.
However, growth in its existing urban markets has started to slow.

Management is considering expanding into smaller Tier-2 and Tier-3 cities.

The expansion would require significant investment in distribution, marketing,
and local partnerships.

The company has two possible approaches.

Option A is to build its own distribution network.

Option B is to partner with existing regional distributors.

Building its own network would provide greater control but require higher
investment.

Using regional distributors would reduce initial investment but could result
in lower control over customer experience and distribution quality.

The management team has not yet decided which approach to take.

The CEO has asked the strategy team to evaluate the options and recommend
a suitable approach.
